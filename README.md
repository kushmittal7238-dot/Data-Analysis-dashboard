# Data-Analysis-dashboard
Interactive Hospital Emergency Room Dashboard in Excel providing insights into patient flow, wait times, admissions, satisfaction, and referrals.

Hospital Emergency Room Dashboard – Executive Overview

This dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of Emergency Room (ER) operations, patient flow, service efficiency, and patient satisfaction. The report covers 131 patient visits and highlights key performance indicators that help hospital management monitor operational effectiveness and patient experience.

Key Performance Metrics
Patient Volume
Total ER Patients: 131
Daily patient visits ranged from 7 to 19 patients per day
Highest patient volume recorded on 11 July (19 patients)
Lowest patient volume recorded on 12 July (7 patients)
Wait Time Analysis
Average Patient Wait Time: 36.98 minutes
Wait times varied between approximately 34–44 minutes
Highest average wait time occurred on 8 July (43.67 minutes)
Patient Satisfaction
Average Satisfaction Score: 6.21 / 10
Satisfaction scores fluctuated significantly across days
Highest satisfaction recorded on 8 July (9.5/10)
Lowest satisfaction recorded on 7 July (4.57/10)
Admission Analysis
Status	Patients	Percentage

The admission ratio indicates that slightly over half of ER visitors were treated and discharged without requiring hospital admission.

Overall Conclusion

The Emergency Room handled 131 patients efficiently with a moderate average wait time of 37 minutes. While patient satisfaction remained reasonable at 6.2/10, the high percentage of delayed attendances (65%) indicates an area for operational improvement. Admission rates were balanced, gender distribution was nearly equal, and most cases were resolved without specialist referrals.

Suggested Improvements
Reduce patient delays through better triage and resource allocation.
Investigate causes of low satisfaction on specific days.
Monitor peak-volume days to prevent increased waiting times.
Continue tracking department referral patterns for staffing optimization.

This dashboard is a strong example of how healthcare analytics can be used to improve patient care, operational efficiency, and decision-making in emergency departments.
